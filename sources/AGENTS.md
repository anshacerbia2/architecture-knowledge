# Source Instructions

Register a source before any claim cites it.

- Prefer standards, original specifications, official security or government
  guidance, and original research for critical claims.
- Evaluate authority in scope; vendor authority about a product is not general
  architecture authority.
- Complete every quality-rubric dimension independently and explain weak,
  unassessed, or not-applicable ratings.
- Record exact version and access date when relevant.
- Mark community articles supplemental unless stronger justification is
  documented.
- `approved` admits a source for evidentiary use only. It does not approve a
  claim or knowledge unit.
- Rejected sources cannot support claims. Restricted sources require explicit
  usage and quotation constraints. Deprecated and superseded sources trigger
  warnings and reassessment.
- Do not copy or store source bodies unless licensing and repository policy
  explicitly permit it.
